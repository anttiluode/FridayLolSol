THE MATHEMATICS
NOT A WIGNER INVERSE
r(t) = Σ 2 xᵢ(t) xⱼ(t)

Cross-multiplication creates sum and difference frequencies. These weren't in either isolated source. The result is zero if either member of a pair is silent.

This is a creative audio instrument inspired by the cross-terms of time-frequency analysis. It is not a literal audible inversion of the Wigner–Ville distribution. Layered songs need not be in the same key or tempo. That mismatch is part of the experiment.

THE LAB NOTEBOOK

All decoding and processing take place locally. No song files are uploaded. The worklet processes stereo audio at your device's sample rate. Maximum 12 tracks. User-supplied music stays in your browser's memory and is cleared on reload.

Try two versions of the same song with a tiny start offset in Phase Weave. Then try two unrelated songs in Cross-Pulse. Next, make a triangle with three different songs and switch to Triple Helix.
